Questions buyers ask

Straight answers

How is hospital revenue leakage calculated?

Estimate gross revenue from beds, occupancy and revenue per occupied bed-day. Apply your charge-capture leakage rate to that. Separately apply your rejection rate to the payer-billed share, then the proportion never recovered. The two together are your annual leakage.

What exactly is charge-capture leakage?

Care that was delivered but never made it onto the bill — a consumable used in theatre, a test performed, a drug administered. The system recorded that the care happened; it just didn't insist the charge follow.

How do you actually reduce claim rejections?

Screen each claim against payer rules, documentation and coding before submission, and flag the ones likely to be rejected so they're fixed first. That shifts denial management from chasing rejections to preventing them.
